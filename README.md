# PocketGenMol: Structure-Conditioned Molecular Generation

## Overview

PocketGenMol is a deep learning framework for structure-based molecular generation, designed to generate drug-like molecules conditioned on 3D protein binding pocket geometry.

Unlike traditional methods that rely on predefined chemical libraries or docking pipelines, this work leverages a masked discrete diffusion model over SAFE molecular representations, combined with a pretrained transformer backbone, to enable end-to-end generative modeling of molecules guided by protein structure.

---

## Key Features

- Structure-conditioned molecular generation using protein pocket geometry  
- SAFE (Sequential Attachment-based Fragment Embedding) representation  
- Masked discrete diffusion for generative modeling  
- Custom PocketEncoder for encoding 3D atomic coordinates and element types  
- Scale-corrected conditioning (alpha = 0.1) for stable training  
- Implemented in PyTorch (no API-based shortcuts)  
- Trained under limited hardware constraints  

---

## Model Architecture

The model consists of three main components:

- Transformer Backbone (GenMol-style)  
  A BERT-like architecture operating on SAFE token sequences for masked token prediction  

- PocketEncoder  
  Encodes protein binding pocket atoms (coordinates + element types) into a 768-dimensional embedding  

- Scale-Corrected Conditioning  
  Pocket embedding is injected into token representations using scaled additive conditioning (alpha = 0.1) to prevent training instability  

- Masked Diffusion Process  
  Molecules are generated by progressively denoising masked SAFE sequences conditioned on pocket embeddings  

---

## Training Setup

Datasets:
- CrossDocked2020 (structure-conditioned training)  
- MOSES (drug-like molecule augmentation)  

Hardware:
- NVIDIA Tesla T4 (16GB, Kaggle environment)  

Training Details:
- 32 epochs  
- Progressive masking curriculum  
- ~16 hours training time  
- Mixed precision (bf16)

## Results

QED (Drug-likeness):        0.681  
Diversity (IntDiv):         0.844  
Uniqueness:                 92.2%  
Validity:                   32%  

- High diversity and uniqueness indicate strong generative capability  
- QED demonstrates drug-like molecular quality  
- Validity remains an area for improvement

---

## Limitations

- Validity (32%) is limited due to SAFE decoding sensitivity  
- Additive conditioning provides coarse structural guidance  
- PocketEncoder does not fully capture geometric invariances  
- Training constrained by compute resources  

---

## Future Work

Future work includes improving conditioning mechanisms through attention or gating, incorporating equivariant geometric models for better pocket representation, improving decoding stability to increase validity, and scaling training to larger datasets and longer durations for better performance.
